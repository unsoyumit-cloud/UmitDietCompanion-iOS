# Recovery Behaviour Catalogue

## Purpose

Help the user recover physically and mentally.

Recovery is more than sleep.

The goal is to improve long-term wellbeing by encouraging appropriate recovery behaviours.

---

# Trigger

Recovery Progress < Expected Progress

---

# Behaviour

recover

---

# Philosophy

Recovery recommendations should encourage rest before performance.

The coach should help users understand that recovery is an active part of a healthy lifestyle.

---

# Behaviour by Day Phase

## 🌅 Morning (06:00 - 11:00)

### Goal

Assess recovery status.

### Action

Encourage an easier day if recovery is low.

---

## ☀️ Midday (11:00 - 15:00)

### Goal

Reduce unnecessary fatigue.

### Action

Encourage hydration and short breaks.

---

## 🌆 Evening (17:00 - 22:00)

### Goal

Prepare the body for recovery.

### Action

Reduce stress and begin winding down.

---

## 🌙 Night (22:00+)

### Goal

Prioritise sleep.

### Action

Encourage bedtime routines.

# Tone of Voice

The coach should:

- Be calm.
- Be reassuring.
- Never pressure the user.
- Encourage rest without creating guilt.
