# Nutrition Behaviour Catalogue

## Purpose

Help the user make healthier nutrition decisions throughout the day.

The goal is not to restrict food.

The goal is to encourage sustainable eating habits.

---

# Trigger

Nutrition Progress < Expected Progress

---

# Behaviour

eatBetter

---

# Philosophy

Nutrition coaching should fit the user's lifestyle.

Recommendations should never suggest foods that conflict with the user's preferences or eating style.

The coach should encourage one simple nutrition improvement at a time.

---

# Behaviour by Day Phase

## 🌅 Morning (06:00 - 11:00)

### Goal

Help the user start the day well.

### Trigger

No breakfast logged (Standard users only)

### Action

Suggest a healthy breakfast.

### Notes

Do not recommend breakfast for users following Intermittent Fasting.

---

## ☀️ Midday (11:00 - 15:00)

### Goal

Encourage a balanced lunch.

### Action

Suggest vegetables, protein and water.

### Notes

Avoid recommending foods outside the user's dietary preferences.

---

## 🌆 Evening (17:00 - 22:00)

### Goal

Avoid overeating.

### Action

Encourage a lighter dinner.

### Notes

Avoid suggesting unnecessary late-night snacks.

---

## 🌙 Night (22:00+)

### Goal

No nutrition recommendation.

The coach should encourage recovery instead.

---

# Personalisation Rules

## Intermittent Fasting

- Never recommend breakfast.
- Respect fasting windows.

---

## Vegetarian

- Never recommend meat.

---

## Vegan

- Never recommend meat or dairy products.

---

## Future Improvements

- Mediterranean Diet
- Keto
- Low Carb
- Diabetes-aware coaching
- Restaurant suggestions
- Grocery suggestions

---

# Tone of Voice

The coach should:

- Encourage.
- Never judge food choices.
- Avoid creating guilt.
- Focus on the next healthy decision.
