# Movement Behaviour Catalogue

## Purpose

Help users reduce sedentary behaviour by encouraging small and achievable movement opportunities throughout the day.

The goal is not to maximise step count.

The goal is to build a sustainable habit of moving regularly.

---

# Trigger

Movement progress below expected level for the current phase of the day.

---

# Behaviour

walk

---

# Philosophy

Movement recommendations should feel achievable.

The coach should suggest actions that can be completed immediately.

Avoid recommending large step goals.

---

# Behaviour by Day Phase

## 🌅 Morning (06:00 - 11:00)

### Goal

Start the day actively.

### Trigger

Movement Progress < 10%

### Action

Take a short walk.

### Friendly Message

🚶 Let's wake the body up with a short walk.

### Funny Message

😄 Coffee is awake.
Maybe your legs should join too.

---

## ☀️ Midday (11:00 - 15:00)

### Goal

Break prolonged sitting.

### Trigger

Movement Progress < 40%

### Action

Walk for 10 minutes after lunch.

### Friendly Message

🚶 A short walk after lunch will help both your body and your mind.

### Funny Message

😄 Your chair has had enough attention today.

---

## 🌇 Afternoon (15:00 - 18:00)

### Goal

Recharge energy.

### Trigger

Movement Progress < 60%

### Action

Stand up and move for a few minutes.

### Friendly Message

🚶 Even a five-minute walk can refresh the rest of your afternoon.

---

## 🌆 Evening (18:00 - 22:00)

### Goal

Reach today's activity target.

### Trigger

Movement Progress < 80%

### Action

Go for a relaxed evening walk.

### Friendly Message

🚶 A relaxing walk is a great way to finish the day.

### Funny Message

😄 Sunset called.
It thinks you'd enjoy a walk.

---

## 🌙 Night (22:00+)

### Goal

No movement recommendation.

Focus on recovery instead.

---

# Anti Rules

Do not recommend movement when:

- The daily movement goal has already been achieved.
- The user is in the sleep period.
- Another higher-priority recommendation is active.

---

# Tone of Voice

- Friendly
- Encouraging
- Action-oriented
- Never judgemental
