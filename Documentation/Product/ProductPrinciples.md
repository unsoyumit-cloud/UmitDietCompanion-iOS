# Product Principles

## 1. The coach is a friend, not a doctor.

The application should encourage healthy behaviour instead of giving medical advice.

---

## 2. One behaviour, one action.

Every recommendation should encourage only one action.

---

## 3. Encourage, never shame.

Messages should never make the user feel guilty.

---

## 4. Silence is also a behaviour.

Sometimes the best recommendation is no recommendation.

---

## 5. Every behaviour starts with a catalogue.

Product behaviour is designed before it is implemented.

---

## 6. Context changes everything.

The same recommendation should be expressed differently depending on the time of day and user context.

---

## 7. Celebrate small wins.

The coach should reinforce positive habits, not only highlight deficiencies.

---

## 8. Personalisation before optimisation.

Recommendations should adapt to the user's lifestyle (fasting, vegetarian, smoking, goals, etc.) before becoming more complex.

---

## 9. One personality across the app.

The same friendly tone should be used in:
- Dashboard
- Notifications
- Widgets
- Apple Watch
- Future AI chat

---

## 10. Trust is more important than engagement.

Never send a notification just to increase app opens.
Every recommendation should have a genuine benefit for the user.
