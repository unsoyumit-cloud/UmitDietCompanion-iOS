# AI Coach Feed

Version: 1.0

Project: Ümit Diet Companion

---

# Vision

The AI Coach Feed is the heart of Ümit Diet Companion.

It is not a dashboard.

It is not a notification center.

It is a daily conversation between the user and their health coach.

Every card must provide value.

If a card has nothing meaningful to say,
it should not exist.

---

# Philosophy

Dashboard shows data.

Coach explains the data.

Dashboard answers:

"What happened?"

Coach answers:

"What does it mean?"

and

"What should I do next?"

---

# Feed Rules

The feed is dynamic.

Cards appear only when they have something valuable to say.

No placeholder cards.

No empty states.

Quality is more important than quantity.

---

# Card Priority

Cards compete for attention.

The feed shows the most valuable cards first.

Maximum:

3 major cards

2 secondary cards

Never overwhelm the user.

---

# Card Types

## 🎯 Today's Focus

Purpose

The single most important action for today.

Rules

Always first.

Only one.

Never replaced.

Examples

Today's focus:

Hydration

Today's focus:

Recovery

Today's focus:

Protein

---

## 💡 Opportunity

Purpose

Suggest the easiest healthy action
based on the current context.

Examples

At grocery store

↓

Buy protein

Coffee shop

↓

Order water too

Gym

↓

Hydrate before training

Office

↓

Take the stairs

Rules

Requires context.

May disappear after use.

---

## 🧠 Insight

Purpose

Help the user understand themselves.

Examples

You consistently drink less water on meeting days.

Your sleep quality improves when you reach your step goal.

You walk much more on weekends.

Rules

Never blame.

Always explain.

Should make the user think:

"I didn't realize that."

---

## 🎉 Celebration

Purpose

Reinforce positive behaviour.

Celebrate

Consistency

Small wins

Streaks

Progress

Never celebrate perfection only.

---

## 📈 Weekly Reflection

Purpose

Summarize the week.

Examples

Biggest success

Most difficult habit

Best improvement

Next week's focus

Appears once per week.

---

## 📅 Monthly Reflection

Purpose

Long-term coaching.

Examples

Monthly trends

Weight progress

Habit changes

Achievements

Next month's priority

Appears once per month.

---

## 🌱 What I'm Learning

Purpose

Show the user what the coach has learned.

Examples

You enjoy evening walks.

Meeting days reduce your hydration.

Celebration motivates you.

Weekend routines are different.

Rules

Only display high-confidence observations.

Never guess.

---

## ❤️ Health Achievement

Purpose

Celebrate milestones.

Examples

First 10,000-step day

Seven-day hydration streak

Five kilograms lost

Thirty days of consistency

Always positive.

# Feed Intelligence

Cards compete.

Example

If an Opportunity card has high value,

↓

an Insight card may be hidden.

If a Celebration card is important,

↓

it may replace a Weekly Reflection.

Every card has a score.

Highest scores appear first.

# User Interaction

Each card can be

Expanded

Saved

Dismissed

Liked

Disliked

Why this?

Dismissing a card teaches the coach.

Positive interaction reinforces future behaviour.

---

# Explainability

Every card should be able to answer:

Why am I seeing this?

Example

Because your hydration usually drops after long meetings.

Because you've completed this goal four days in a row.

Because today is your grocery shopping day.

Trust comes from transparency.

---

# AI Principles

The coach should never interrupt without value.

The coach should never repeat itself unnecessarily.

The coach should never create guilt.

The coach should always create understanding.

---

# Success Metric

The user should open the feed because they are curious.

Not because they feel guilty.

The ideal reaction is:

"I wonder what my coach noticed today."

Not:

"I wonder what I'm doing wrong."
