# Water Behaviour Catalogue

## Purpose

Help the user maintain healthy hydration throughout the day by suggesting one simple action at the right moment.

The goal is **not** to remind users to drink water constantly.

The goal is to help them naturally reach their daily hydration target.

---

# Trigger

Water Progress below target.

---

# Behaviour

drinkWater

---

# Philosophy

Hydration coaching should feel supportive.

The coach should never repeatedly remind users to drink water.

Instead, every recommendation should provide context and encourage one simple action.

---

# Behaviour by Day Phase

## 🌅 Morning (06:00 - 11:00)

### Goal

Start the day well hydrated.

### Trigger

Water Progress < 20%

### Action

Drink one glass of water.

### Friendly Message

💧 Good morning! Shall we start the day with a glass of water?

### Funny Message

😄 Your coffee has probably arrived already...
Let's invite some water too.

---

## ☀️ Midday (11:00 - 17:00)

### Goal

Stay on track.

### Trigger

Water Progress < 50%

### Action

Drink one glass of water.

### Friendly Message

💧 You're a little behind today's water target.
One glass now will make the afternoon much easier.

### Funny Message

😄 Your water bottle looks lonely.
Maybe it's waiting for you.

---

## 🌆 Evening (17:00 - 22:00)

### Goal

Finish today's target.

### Trigger

Water Progress < 80%

### Action

Complete today's hydration target.

### Friendly Message

💧 Only a little more to reach today's goal.

### Funny Message

😄 Today's finish line is surprisingly close.
Two glasses and you're there.

---

## 🌙 Night (22:00+)

### Goal

No recommendation.

Avoid encouraging excessive water intake before bedtime.

---

# Anti Rules

Do not recommend drinking water when:

- Water goal has already been achieved.
- It is late at night.
- The user has already received multiple hydration recommendations today.

---

# Tone of Voice

The coach should:

- Be encouraging.
- Be light-hearted.
- Never shame the user.
- Never repeatedly send the same reminder.
