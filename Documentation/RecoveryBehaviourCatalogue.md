# SleepBehaviourCatalogue Rule

## Purpose

Help the user recover from insufficient sleep by suggesting one actionable behaviour at the right time of the day.

The goal is **not** to make the user feel guilty.
The goal is to help the user make a better decision today.

---

# Trigger

Sleep Progress < 70%

---

# Behaviour

sleepEarlier

---

# Philosophy

This rule follows the **Action First Coaching** principle.

The coach should never simply report a metric.

Every recommendation must answer:

1. What is the problem?
2. What is the best action right now?
3. Why is that action important?

---

# Behaviour by Day Phase

## 🌅 Morning (06:00 - 11:00)

### Goal

Create awareness and help the user plan the evening.

### Action

Plan to sleep earlier tonight.

### Friendly Message

😴 You slept a little less than your target last night.
Let's aim for an earlier bedtime tonight.

### Funny Message

😄 Looks like your sleep clock clocked out a little early.
Let's get it back on track tonight.

---

## ☀️ Midday (11:00 - 17:00)

### Goal

No recommendation.

Sleep can no longer be improved today.

The coach should focus on Water, Movement or Nutrition instead.

---

## 🌆 Evening (17:00 - 22:00)

### Goal

Prepare the user for sleep.

### Action

Reduce screen time and start winding down.

### Friendly Message

🌙 Tonight is a good opportunity to recover some of yesterday's sleep.

### Funny Message

📺 Netflix will still be there tomorrow.
Your sleep won't wait that long. 😄

---

## 🌙 Night (22:00+)

### Goal

Encourage immediate action.

### Action

Go to bed.

### Friendly Message

🌙 Your body will thank you if you head to bed now.

### Funny Message

📱 Your phone is fully charged.
Now it's your turn. 😄

---

# Multi-Day Behaviour

## 3 Days

### Trigger

Sleep deficit for 3 consecutive days.

### Message

😴 You've been sleeping a little less than usual lately.
Let's try to recover this weekend.

---

## 5 Days

### Trigger

Sleep deficit for 5 consecutive days.

### Message

🌙 You've built up some sleep debt this week.
A longer night this weekend could really help.

---

## 10 Days

### Trigger

Sleep deficit for 10 consecutive days.

### Message

❤️ You've been struggling with sleep for a while.
If this continues, it might be worth discussing it with a healthcare professional.

The app never provides medical advice.
It only encourages professional consultation when appropriate.

---

# Tone of Voice

The coach should:

- Be supportive.
- Be friendly.
- Use light humour.
- Never judge.
- Never create guilt.
- Never sound like a doctor.
